Before you raise a ticket

Most requests to the MST IT Service Desk are solved in a few minutes with the steps below. If they don't help, raise a ticket and include the details listed under "What to include".

I forgot my password or my account is locked
Use self-service password reset from the sign-in page and select "Forgot my password". You need the phone number or authenticator app registered on your account. Locked accounts unlock automatically after 15 minutes.
VPN connects but I can't open internal sites
Disconnect, restart the VPN client and connect again. If you are on hotel or guest Wi-Fi, open any website first to accept the network's terms page, then connect the VPN.
Teams calls drop or the audio is poor
Close other video apps, switch from Wi-Fi to a cable if you can, and check the device selected under Teams Settings, Devices. Raise a ticket if it happens on every call.
The office printer shows offline
Remove the printer from Settings, Printers and add it again from the company portal. Each office has its own printer queue, so pick the one for your floor.
OneDrive shows a red cross on files
Open the OneDrive icon in the taskbar and read the error. File names with characters such as " * : < > ? / \ | or paths longer than 400 characters do not sync.
I need software that isn't installed
Check the Software Library in the company portal first. Licensed software is requested through a ticket with your manager's approval.

No self-help article matches. Raise a ticket and describe the problem.

Incident

Something that used to work is broken: no email, laptop won't start, an app shows an error.

Service request

Something new: a laptop for a new starter, access to a shared mailbox, a software licence.

Access request

Access to a SharePoint site or Team. Ask the site owner first; IT only handles it if there is no owner.

Change request

A change to a system used by others. Goes through the IT Change Requests process and weekly approval.
